Orthodontic Treatments for Children, Teens, and Adults

Modern orthodontics offers several treatment options to suit different dental needs, lifestyles, and age groups. The best choice depends on the patient's teeth, bite condition, and treatment goals.

Traditional Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces are a reliable option for correcting many orthodontic problems. They use brackets and wires to gradually move teeth into better positions. They can address crowded teeth, spacing problems, and different bite irregularities.

Clear Braces

Clear braces work similarly to traditional braces but use less noticeable brackets. They are a popular option for patients who want effective orthodontic treatment with a more discreet appearance.

Invisalign and Clear Aligners

Clear aligners offer a removable alternative to traditional braces for suitable patients. These custom-made trays gradually move teeth into their desired positions. Patients can remove them when eating, drinking certain beverages, brushing, and flossing.

Early Orthodontic Treatment

Children may benefit from an orthodontic evaluation while their teeth and jaws are still developing. Early assessment can help identify potential alignment and bite problems and determine whether treatment is needed.

How to Maintain Your Smile During Orthodontic Treatment

Taking care of your teeth during orthodontic treatment is essential for achieving healthy results. Patients with braces should brush carefully around brackets and wires to remove food particles and plaque. Flossing daily is also important for keeping the spaces between teeth clean.

If you use clear aligners, follow your orthodontist's instructions about wearing and cleaning them. Attend scheduled appointments so your treatment progress can be monitored and adjustments can be made when necessary.

After completing orthodontic treatment, wearing retainers as recommended helps maintain the new position of your teeth and reduces the risk of unwanted movement.
